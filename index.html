Competition Entry Visual Design Cultural Storytelling 🏆 2nd Place

Al Ameed Drive Through

A competition entry for Al Ameed Coffee's drive-through experience — a cinematic visual journey through Jordanian civilisation, flowing from north to south through cultural motifs, heritage architecture, and symbolic storytelling. Awarded 2nd Place.

Scroll to explore
1 / 3
01

The Concept

Designed for a drive-through branding competition hosted by Al Ameed Coffee, the concept translates Jordanian civilisation into a continuous visual narrative — guiding the visitor's eye from north to south through the country's most iconic cultural landmarks and motifs.

02

Visual Language

Environmental graphics drew from traditional Jordanian patterns, architectural heritage, and natural landscapes. The flow of imagery was intentionally designed as a journey — immersive, symbolic, and culturally resonant for every visitor passing through.

03

Result

The design was recognised with Second Place in the competition — a validation of both the cultural depth and the visual craft invested in the project. Merchandise concepts, including illustrated tote bags, were developed as extensions of the visual identity.
